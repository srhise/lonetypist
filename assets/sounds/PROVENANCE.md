# Sound provenance

Every file here is cut from recordings of a Hermes Precisa 305 manual
typewriter by Joseph Sardin, published on BigSoundBank
(https://bigsoundbank.com) under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication).
Each source page states "CC0 (public domain): Free and royalty-free".
CC0 asks for nothing; the credit here is a courtesy.

Source pages are `https://bigsoundbank.com/detail-<number>.html`.

Each key strike runs from 8ms before its peak to 120ms after, so the
click lands with the key rather than after it; each sound has a short
fade at both ends, is mixed to mono, normalised to 85% of full scale,
and stored as 16-bit 48kHz PCM.
